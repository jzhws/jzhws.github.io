About me

I am a Ph.D. student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and a Research Intern at Shanghai AI Laboratory.

My research focuses on visual quality assessment, large multimodal models, and large model evaluation. I study how multimodal models perceive, explain, and reason about the quality of images and videos, with an emphasis on pre-training and post-training, including reinforcement fine-tuning. I also develop benchmarks and evaluation methods to assess the capabilities and limitations of large models.

I am also interested in evaluating AI-generated visual content and understanding human perceptual and emotional responses to images.

I am currently seeking research internship opportunities. Please feel free to connect with me if you are interested in my research or would like to explore potential collaborations.

How can we build multimodal models that understand visual quality as well as they understand visual content?
